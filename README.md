# Book Owl

> Read, tap, get wise. Hoot. Repeat.

Book Owl is a quiet EPUB reader with local dictionaries, collections, and a saved vocabulary. It keeps your books and reading data on your device. EPUB only · No accounts · No uploads.

[Open Book Owl](https://dimitrov-i.github.io/book-owl/)

## A short field guide

> **Field note.** If a word looks suspicious, tap it. If it keeps turning up, save it.
- **Read.** Open an EPUB from Library. Book Owl saves it to your device before opening it. Tap its cover later to resume where you left off.
- **Organize.** Press and hold a cover for book information and collection actions. A book can belong to several collections.
- **Look up words.** Import local StarDict files under **References → Dictionaries**. Select a word while reading to look it up, then choose **Save word** to keep it in **Vocabulary**.
- **Make yourself comfortable.** Switch between paginated and scrolling layouts, adjust the font and text size, or choose Light, Sepia, Dark, or System.
- **Take it offline.** On iPhone, open the official site in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

Book Owl handles EPUBs conservatively. Some complex layouts and interactive features may display differently. JavaScript packaged in an EPUB never runs.

This app does not include any book or dictionary data. You must import your own files.

## Privacy respected

Book Owl stores your books, dictionaries, saved words, reading progress, and settings locally. The app uses no analytics, telemetry, or cookies; it does not process any user information; and it does not upload your files or reading data.
